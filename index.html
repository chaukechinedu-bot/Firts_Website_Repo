Local Students Embrace Digital Study Tools to Improve Academic Performance

In an increasingly digital world, students across South Africa are turning to online tools and applications to enhance their learning experience and improve academic performance. With the rise of mobile technology and accessible internet, digital study platforms have become an essential part of modern education. Many students, particularly those preparing for major examinations, are now using web-based study trackers, flashcard apps, and online tutorials to organize their schedules and retain information more effectively. These tools allow learners to create structured study plans, monitor their progress, and stay motivated throughout their academic journey
